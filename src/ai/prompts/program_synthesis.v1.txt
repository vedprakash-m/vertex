Respond with JSON only, matching this shape:

{
  "through_line": "one paragraph, plain language, no bullet formatting",
  "long_poles": ["short phrase identifying a long-pole item", "..."],
  "facts": ["a directly observed fact, stated plainly", "..."],
  "inferences": ["a reasoned conclusion drawn from one or more facts", "..."],

    {"text": "a specific decision or action", "evidence_refs": ["item_id_1", "item_id_2"]}
  ]
}

Rules:
- Every recommendation.evidence_refs entry must be an item_id that appears in INPUT ITEMS.
- If you cannot support a recommendation with at least one evidence_ref, omit it rather

- through_line must never be empty.
