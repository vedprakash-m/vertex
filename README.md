# Vertex

**The Governed Program-Intelligence Platform for Technical Program Managers (TPMs) and Engineering Managers (EMs).**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stop chasing status. Align delivery with executive trust.**
> Vertex transforms fragmented delivery evidence from work trackers, telemetry databases, incident portals, and communication feeds into a single, continuous, and highly auditable program-intelligence model. It projects this model into multiple decision-ready surfaces—newsletters, daily digests, executive decks, and engineering hygiene nudges—while keeping TPMs and EMs firmly in the editorial driver's seat.
>
> **Current supported scope:** Vertex supports **any Microsoft TPM program** within the declared supported archetypes and exclusions. **Roadmap direction:** broader TPM/EM adoption anywhere in the world and additional non-ADO ecosystems/toolsets (for example Jira and Slack) are intentional expansion targets, but they are not part of the current supported bar.

---

## 🚀 Why Vertex?

High-performance product delivery is often slowed down by the friction between **program coordination** and **engineering execution**:
* **TPMs** spend hours chasing updates across teams, copying fields from tickets, and manually compiling executive newsletters.
* **EMs** struggle to ensure that tracking systems (Jira*/ADO) accurately reflect real engineering activity, resulting in stale data, missed dependencies, and silent telemetry slips.

**Vertex bridges this gap. It maintains a single program-intelligence model and projects it at the exact altitudes that leaders, program managers, and engineering leads need:**

```text
  [ Raw Program Evidence ]  ──( Telemetry, Trackers, Chat, Incidents )
             │
             ▼
      [ Gather Phase ]      ──( Normalize into Append-Only Signal Journal )
             │
             ▼
   [ Program Model Plane ]  ──( Delta Engines, Forecasts, Trajectory Analysis )
             │
             ▼
     [ Triage & Review ]    ──( TPM & EM-in-the-Loop Joint Judgment )
             │
             ▼
     [ Multi-Altitude ]     ──( Glance: 10s Banner | Scan: 60s Scorecard | Deep: Narratives )
             │
             ▼
   [ Governed Execution ]   ──( Outlook HTML, Teams cards, Decks, Hygiene Nudges, Proposals )
```

## ✨ Core Pillars

### 1. The Three-Speed Reading Model
Every report generated by Vertex is automatically structured for three reading speeds, ensuring executive sponsors, program leads, and engineering directors get value within their exact time budget:

| Speed | Target Time | Visual Surface | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🔴 **Glance** | **≤10 seconds** | Health Banner & Nav Bar | High-impact, color-coded risk indicators that require no reading. |
| 🟡 **Scan** | **≤60 seconds** | Top 3 Highlights, What Changed & Scorecards | Delta-first summaries, engineering metrics, and telemetry trends. |
| 🟢 **Deep Dive** | **2–8 minutes** | Executive Summaries & Workstream Sections | Rich narrative updates with inline source-traceable evidence and citations. |

### 2. Delta-First Engineering Awareness
Vertex focuses heavily on **change and trajectory** rather than static status:
* **Drift & Oscillation Tracking:** Detects work items experiencing chronic target-date slipping, ownership transitions, and state oscillations.
* **Telemetry-Backed Validation:** Compares tracker states (Jira*/ADO) against telemetry (Kusto/BigQuery*) to catch silent delivery gaps before they become incidents.
* **Fail-Loud Source Health:** Every scorecard dimension declares a source contract (required sources, freshness, minimum yield). A required source that is missing, stale, or zero-yield is made visible and blocking — or explicitly waived with owner and reason — never silently absorbed. All functions (newsletter, nudges, risk register, reviews) project from one source-traceable program fact store rather than re-deriving state.
* **Author Overrides:** Derived risk levels are calculated programmatically from code/issue behavior, but authors apply overrides to add human judgment.
* **Narrative Delta Hints:** The `ado_narrative_hint_engine` continuously compares ADO evidence against authored narratives and surfaces targeted, per-workstream improvement hints (`vertex hints`) for author accept/reject/modify — keeping narratives current without manual drift detection.
* **Governance Tracking:** Decision records, DFD dates, and escalation state are tracked as structured data (`vertex decisions governance`) and surface as annotated scorecard badges, keeping leadership alignment visible in every publication cycle.

> \* **Planned integrations:** Jira and BigQuery adapters are on the roadmap via the Unified Integration Layer. Current shipping adapters are ADO and Kusto (see [Ecosystem & Integration Layer](#-ecosystem--integration-layer) below).

### 3. Developer Hygiene & Vitality Loops
Vertex helps EMs maintain healthy tracking practices without manual nagging:
* **Vitality & Freshness Scores:** Measures work items on a 0–100 composite scale based on update frequency, comment richness, and cross-channel activity.
* **Automated Nudges:** Generates drafted, contextual, and polite email notifications (or Teams card alerts) to help item owners update stale cards ahead of publication loops.
  Nudge drafts stay human-sent, honor audience-policy guardrails, support `--approve-draft` / `--import-sent`, and only start cooldown after send attestation or import.

### 4. Program Event Ledger
Vertex maintains an append-only, hash-chained event log (`programs/<id>/ledger/`) that captures longitudinal program reality — milestone changes, risk events, dependency status, operator decisions — as typed, bi-temporal, source-traceable events:
* **52 Event Types** across 7 categories (Identity, Structure, Reality, Observation, Knowledge, Meta/Control, Discovery). Every event carries provenance, a confidence tier, and a content hash.
* **Replayable Projections:** `vertex ledger replay` rebuilds the current program state deterministically from the event log at any point in time.
* **Discovery Pipelines:** AI extractors and M365 connectors stage candidates; `vertex ledger triage` governs their lifecycle before events land in the authoritative log.
* **Compliance Redaction:** `vertex ledger redact` performs targeted §10.8 payload scrubs while preserving hash-chain continuity via a redaction registry.
* **Knowledge Plane:** `vertex knowledge` manages scope-tagged claims (program/domain/portfolio) with a closed predicate vocabulary, content-addressed vault, and `knowledge_context()` time-travel queries.

### 5. Governed Context Updates
Vertex can stage and apply context updates from confirmed evidence, but only through approved local write paths. In v1, `vertex context apply` is limited to assumptions, decisions, milestones, risks, and workstreams; each apply checks a fresh hash, records a changelog/ledger entry, and can recover from partial writes.

## 🛡️ The Autonomy Ladder (Maturity Levels)

We believe automation must be earned. Vertex includes a **5-Level Autonomy Ladder** that gates system authority based on historical trust, safety checks, and user verification. This ensures EMs and TPMs can scale from basic deterministic compiles to automated hygiene nudge campaigns without giving up control:

| Level | Profile | System Authority | Human Role | Promotion Criteria |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L0** | **Deterministic Compiler** | Formats, renders, and validates deterministic inputs. | Manually drives every step. | Baseline state. |
| **L1** | **Advisory Detection** | Surfaces anomalies, contradictions, and stale commitments. | Reviews recommendations. | Acceptable false-positive rate over 5 sessions. |
| **L2** | **AI-Assisted Proposal Staging** | Drafts narratives, comments, briefs, and tag proposals locally. | Explicitly approves or rejects each staged item. | Prior proposal acceptance rate ≥ 70%. |
| **L3** | **Governed Bounded Writes** | Automatically applies approved updates within a locked blast radius. | Approves batches; retains full rollback control. | Blast radius defined, rollback exists, acceptance ≥ 90%. |
| **L4** | **Scheduled Low-Risk Autonomy** | Executes standing-policy writes (e.g., hygiene nudges) on a timer. | Monitors audit logs and holds a "kill switch." | Proven at L3 for ≥10 cycles; zero recent contradictions. |

## 🔒 Local-First Security & Data Privacy

Engineering Managers and compliance officers love Vertex because it is built around strict, local-first data isolation:

* **Zero-SaaS Footprint:** Vertex is a local CLI developer tool, not a hosted service. No webhooks, no daemon processes, and absolutely no external SaaS databases holding your program's narrative state.
* **Secure AI Processing:** Optional AI blurb generation runs through your secure, enterprise-provisioned Azure OpenAI endpoint with strict token context budgets. Your program's internal emails, chat logs, and code directories are never leaked to public model training sets.
* **Ingestion-Time PII Scrubbing:** All harvested collaboration signals (chat threads, emails) are scrubbed of personal addresses, phone numbers, and emails at the ingest boundary before they are written to the local signal journal.

### 1. Included Microsoft Adapter Pack
Vertex ships with an out-of-the-box, enterprise-grade adapter pack for the Microsoft ecosystem:
* **Work Tracking:** Azure DevOps (ADO) OData/REST query hydration, revision history parsing, and secure, concurrent tag/comment write-backs.
* **Telemetry & Observability:** Azure Data Explorer (Kusto) query execution, binding raw metrics directly to program KPIs. Graph API consent is not required for the default path — ingestion uses local desktop exports.
* **AI Assistance:** Optional Azure OpenAI integration for secure, grounded blurb and executive summary drafts behind a 4-stage safety pipeline.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### 1. Prerequisites
* Python **3.11** or higher.
* Operating System: Windows (optimized with cross-platform `portalocker` file locking), macOS, or Linux.
* Git.

### 2. Install
Clone the repository and initialize the Python virtual environment:
```bash
# Create and activate virtual environment
python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate  # On Windows: .\.venv\Scripts\activate

# Install dependencies and editable platform package
pip install -r requirements.txt
pip install -e . --no-deps
```

Configure your local environment by copying the example:
```bash
cp .env.example .env
# Edit .env to add your credentials (PATs, API keys, endpoints)
```

Canonical specs live in `specs/vertex-prd.md`, `specs/vertex-tech-spec.md`, and `specs/vertex-ux-spec.md`. Other spec drafts are local-only and intentionally ignored.

### 3. Verify
```bash
pytest tests/ -q
```

## ⚡ Quick Start

For M365-backed newsletters, the durable operator loop is `vertex gather` → `vertex enrich` → review pending M365 signals → `vertex report`. When an edition sets `m365.workiq_enrich_schedule: pre_report`, `vertex report` automatically runs `vertex enrich` first unless `--offline` is used.

WorkIQ discovery is rollback-safe by default (`legacy_nl`); qualified programs can opt into bounded structured enumeration. Configuration and safety details live in the [technical specification](specs/vertex-tech-spec.md#1311-workiq-retrieval-contract).

### Option A — See it in 30 seconds (no credentials needed)

Run the interactive demo. No files are written; it just shows you exactly what setup produces for a fictional program:
```bash
vertex setup --demo
```

### Option B — Set up your own program (5 minutes)

`vertex setup` is a conversational, AI-assisted wizard. It asks a few questions, optionally queries your ADO workspace, proposes a workstream structure, and writes all config files after you confirm:
```bash
vertex setup
```

Type `?` at any prompt for contextual help. Press `Ctrl-C` to cancel safely at any point — no files are written until you confirm.

**Flags you'll use most:**
```bash
vertex setup --demo                         # walkthrough with a fictional program
vertex setup --from-description "..."       # skip greeting; AI infers workstreams
vertex setup --manual                       # skip ADO discovery; guided questions only
vertex setup --dry-run                      # preview the generated YAML without writing
vertex setup --resume                       # continue an interrupted session
```

### Option C — AI-assisted from a one-liner

If `VERTEX_AI_DEPLOYMENT` or `AZURE_OPENAI_DEPLOYMENT` is set, Vertex will suggest workstreams automatically:
```bash
vertex setup --from-description "Weekly reliability report for the Platform team shipping in Q3"
```

## 🔄 The Full Authoring Loop

After setup, the weekly workflow is:
```bash
vertex reality status --program my_program  # daily front door: SoR mode, conflicts, truth distribution
vertex doctor --edition my_weekly            # validate config, schemas, source health
vertex gather --program my_program           # ingest live tracker & telemetry data
vertex gather --program my_program --sharepoint --lt-deck  # also gather SharePoint ref docs & LT deck (optional)
vertex enrich --edition my_weekly            # extract structured WorkstreamEvidence from transcripts (optional, when WorkIQ signals present)
vertex triage --full --edition my_weekly     # prioritized checklist: all attention items, conflicts, pending actuations
vertex override --edition my_weekly          # apply human judgment to risk levels
vertex report --edition my_weekly            # draft and preview the newsletter
vertex review-full --edition my_weekly       # DRI section sign-off
vertex confirm --edition my_weekly           # archive snapshot and extract commitments
```

If you're using M365 signals, authored meeting/chat/email names are treated as discovery intent. `vertex gather` will auto-resolve unique high-confidence matches and queue the rest for `vertex integration candidates --program my_program --requires-decision`; `vertex doctor --operator-gates --edition my_weekly` tells you whether the next step is PM review, manual seeding, auth/admin work, or config cleanup.

Vertex runs entirely on-demand via the CLI — no daemon, no auto-send. The fastest daily entry points:

```bash
vertex reality status --program my_program  # SoR mode, conflicts, truth distribution
vertex doctor    --edition my_weekly        # config/schema/source-health validation
```

For the full weekly authoring loop (`gather` → `enrich` → `override` → `report` → `review-full` → `confirm`) and all flags, see [The Full Authoring Loop](#-the-full-authoring-loop) above and the `vertex --help` tree. ## 🔒 High-Confidence Security & Design Invariants

Vertex is built to be secure, audit-friendly, and enterprise-resilient:
* **The Sacred Zone Boundary:** Enforced by AST-level unit tests, `src/core/` can **never** import from `src/ai/` (probabilistic AI) or `src/m365/` (integrations). This ensures the core analytical loop remains completely isolated and deterministic.
* **Single Write Path:** Only `snapshot_store.write_confirmed()` can write confirmed issues to the program archive, preventing draft generations from polluting program history.
* **PII Scrubber:** Collaboration signals are scrubbed of personal addresses, phone numbers, and emails at ingestion time.
* **Echo-Chamber Guard:** Discovery algorithms skip work item revisions authored by Vertex itself during write-back operations.
* **Outlook-Safe Risk Rendering:** Risk chips and health banners use table-based inline CSS with fixed foreground/background color pairs selected for AA contrast compliance across Outlook Desktop, OWA, and mobile clients.
* **Supply-Chain Hardening:** `pip-audit --strict` runs in CI on every push; HMAC archive-signing (QG-26) guards the confirmed-issue archive. Threat model, AI model lifecycle, and privacy data-classification matrix are in `governance/`.

## 🗺️ Product Direction

Vertex represents the future of program-intelligence and delivery execution. By separating raw engineering activity logs from high-level program models, it empowers **TPMs and EMs** to keep engineering teams aligned and leadership informed with zero manual overhead.

The deterministic platform milestone is complete: Vertex maintains a continuously validated program reality model with 5 truth levels, cross-source conflict detection, governed context updates, and a `vertex triage --full` daily front door for every attention item. All coding-implementable work is done, including the read-path migration that lets accepted REV facts flow into rendered output per authority family — and, as of 2026-07-07, the full REV loop has been proven end-to-end on real data: a human-approved fact from a real email now appears, cited and reverse-linkable, in a real render.

Remaining production-readiness work is operator/human-paced, not engineering: dual-annotator corpus certification (κ) before broader authority promotion, and IT provisioning of Azure Content Safety for non-degraded cycle authority. See `specs/backlog.md` for the current, executable-gate-mapped status of this remaining work.

Next directions: broader TPM/EM adoption outside Microsoft, additional non-ADO ecosystems (Jira, Slack), and the actuation/autonomy ladder progression (L1 → L2 proposal staging) once the operator-gate proof cycles complete.
