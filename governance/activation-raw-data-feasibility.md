# Activation Raw-Data Feasibility

**Scope:** Evidence requirements for an explicitly selected program.

Before activation, record the selected claim family, its bound accessor,
reachable source-document count and required activation floor in private
operator evidence. Obtain documents only through approved source access.

Do not mark `P-1-RAW-DATA` green until the live verifier reports enough reachable
documents for the selected family. If no family reaches its configured floor,
acquisition or a separately approved scope decision precedes authority promotion.
This public procedure contains no deployment counts or activation certification.
